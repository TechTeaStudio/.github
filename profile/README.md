## What we are building

Our flagship is **Hyperion Omni Client** — and the simplest way to explain it is by example.

Imagine three customers who, on paper, have nothing in common:

- A **meat factory** that needs to monitor ovens, control them through buttons, and stream video from the floor.
- A **school** that needs a Google Classroom-style platform with lessons, assignments, video calls, and user management.
- A **medical facility** that needs an ERP-grade system to manage patients, with a powerful query layer underneath.

To the rest of the industry, these are three different applications, three different budgets, three different multi-year rewrites. **In the Hyperion mindset, they are the same application** - driven by different backends and assembled from different plugins. The Users plugin a school needs is the same Users plugin a hospital needs. The query system a clinic needs is the same one a bank needs. The video-stream control on a factory floor is the same control a school uses for its meetings.

This is achieved through WebAssembly. Pre-made controls are already convenient — but our ambition reaches further: **pre-made backends and pre-made functionality to go with them.** Big companies attempt "box" solutions and stop at their own product line. We are taking the same idea wider: one platform, infinite verticals, real reuse.

A simple goal — *a UI client for any HTTP server* — polished into a real, ambitious solution.
